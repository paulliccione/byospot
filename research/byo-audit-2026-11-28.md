A venue stays on the live list only when its own website confirms BYO, or a phone call already confirmed it. After these counts are approved, guide-only venues would be removed from `data.js`. ## How the check was done

Every place in `data.js` is one row (1044 venues). The slug is the same id the site builds from the name and suburb.

Where a venue has its own website, the check requested the homepage and, when those links were on the site, one menu page, one drinks page, one FAQ, one bookings page, one about page, one menu PDF linked from the site, and one Instagram or Facebook profile. That is at most eight requests for a venue. Requests used a normal browser user agent, waited between requests, kept to two at a time per host (one at a time for Instagram, Facebook, Quandoo, and Time Out), and skipped URLs disallowed by robots.txt. Pages that timed out, returned an error, or showed a block page are `fetch_failed/blocked`.

Confirmation is wording on the venue's own site (`sourceType` site), a menu PDF linked from that site (`pdf`), or the venue's own Instagram or Facebook (`social`). A social account counts when the venue's site links to it, or when the stored website is that account and the handle or profile name matches the venue. The `socialReason` column says which of those applied. In this check every Instagram and Facebook profile redirected to a login wall, so no social page supplied wording and none is counted as confirmation. Time Out, Quandoo, findbyo, AGFG, Tripadvisor, IWFS, Vino Cammino, SevenRooms, NowBookIt, OpenTable, and other guides, directories, or booking widgets do not confirm a venue. A line that only welcomes BYO cake, and a customer review that merely exclaims that a place is BYO, are not counted. A line that says there is no BYO charge is counted as free BYO.

The wording looked for was BYO, bring your own wine or drinks, and corkage, plus an explicit refusal (`no BYO`, `not BYO`, `fully licensed, no BYO`). A limit such as "no BYO on Friday" is a confirmation with a day limit, because the venue still offers BYO at other times. A sentence that says the venue is not a BYO venue and then says guests may BYO on a stated day is a confirmation with that limit. A page that only says "fully licensed", with no BYO words, is not a refusal. If separate pages disagreed, or the only line was "call to confirm BYO" without saying BYO is allowed, the status is `own_site_no_mention`. Nothing was filled in from memory. `byoDays` is a short phrase copied from beside the venue's own wording. It is empty when the venue's pages did not state a day or time limit. A range such as Wednesday to Sunday covers the days inside it when comparing notes. Lunch or dinner in a note is treated as found when the venue states a midday or evening hour beside the BYO line.

`claimSources` is only what the current note or corkage field already names. Notes that say "BYO listed but not confirmed" do not name a guide, so those venues are not called findbyo and they are not in the guide-only removal list.
